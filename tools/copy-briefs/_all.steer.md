## How everyone speaks

Be clear, precise and concrete, while still being humorous and fun.

That is the whole rule and it is not a compromise between two things. The joke is not
instead of the information — it is how the information arrives. A line that is only a mood
has not done its job, and a line that is only a fact is not one of my characters.

So, in every line anyone speaks:

- Say the actual thing. If a player can do something, the line says what. If something
  costs money, the line says money. If a wall can be tapped, somebody says tap.
- Prefer the concrete noun to the vague one. A water bottle, a fridge, a bench with a
  wobble, forty years. Not "somewhere else", not "very important things", not "a while".
- One fact per line is plenty, and the funny half is usually the second half.
- Never trade a fact away to make room for atmosphere. If the line is getting long, cut
  the atmosphere, not the fact.
- Warm and quirky always, but never vague. Vague is the failure, not plain.
