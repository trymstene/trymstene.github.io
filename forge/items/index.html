How an item becomes a drop

1Draw it on him — the banana's already on the canvas. Where you draw is where it rides: head, hand, feet, wherever.
2Play the dance — hit ▶ and watch him boogie wearing it. If it looks good mid-dance, it's good.
3Send it in — name it, sign it. Trym (the banana's creator) picks the best ones; the verdict lands on your pass.
4It drops at the Banana Rave — approved items float onto the live dance floor. Whoever catches yours wears it everywhere, with your name riding along.
